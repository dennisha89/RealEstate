# LootVue Brand Voice Guide

## Identity
LootVue is the sharp analyst friend who left Goldman to help regular investors stop getting fleeced. Confident, data-driven, provocative — never corporate, never generic.

## Tone Spectrum
- **Headlines**: Provocative, confrontational. Challenge the status quo. "Your broker profits from what you don't know."
- **Feature copy**: Confident, specific. Lead with the metric, not the adjective. "6 correlated stress scenarios simultaneously" not "advanced stress testing."
- **Data labels**: Clinical, precise. "DSCR 1.35x" not "Good debt coverage."
- **CTAs**: Action-oriented, urgent. "Analyze Your First Deal Free" not "Get Started."
- **Error states**: Honest, helpful. "Unable to compute — insufficient comparable data" not "Oops! Something went wrong."

## Core Phrases (Use)
- "Information asymmetry" — the core problem we solve
- "Institutional-grade" — what the product delivers
- "What Blackstone sees" — the aspiration
- "Same depth. Fraction of the cost." — the value prop
- "Your broker hopes you never find this" — the hook
- "12 engines. One verdict." — the product summary
- "Confidence percentage" — how we express certainty (never just "AI says buy")

## Banned Phrases (Never Use)
- "AI-powered" / "leveraging AI" — overused, meaningless
- "Revolutionary" / "game-changing" — empty superlatives
- "Best-in-class" / "world-class" — unverifiable
- "Seamless" / "frictionless" — everyone says it
- "Disrupt" — cringe in 2026
- "One-stop shop" — sounds like a gas station
- "Take your investing to the next level" — LinkedIn energy

## Number Formatting
- Always use specific numbers over vague claims: "$48,000" not "tens of thousands"
- Financial metrics in mono font, tabular nums
- Percentages to 1 decimal: "7.2%" not "~7%"
- Large currency: compact on cards ("$285K"), full in prose ("$285,000")
- Negative values: rose + parentheses: "($2,500)" not "-$2,500"

## Color-as-Language
- **Gold** (#C9A227): Primary brand, premium, LootVue itself
- **Emerald** (#10B981): Buy signal, positive cash flow, good metric, profit
- **Amber** (#F59E0B): Hold/caution, needs attention, mixed signal
- **Rose** (#EF4444): Avoid signal, negative cash flow, risk, loss

## Audience Tiers (Adapt Copy)
1. **First-time investors**: Explain every term. "Cap rate (how much the property earns relative to its price): 7.2%"
2. **Portfolio owners**: Skip basics. Lead with portfolio-level impact. "This acquisition shifts your portfolio DSCR from 1.28x to 1.31x"
3. **Institutional/PE**: Speak their language. Debt yield, yield-on-cost, exit cap sensitivity. No hand-holding.

## Landing Page Copy Rules
- Lead with the uncomfortable truth, not the product
- Every claim backed by a specific number
- Competitor comparisons: name them directly (Zillow, DealCheck, CoStar)
- Testimonials must include dollar amounts or time saved — no "great tool!" fluff
- Pricing positioned against institutional alternatives ($24K Bloomberg, $15K CoStar)
- CTAs repeat the "free" message — reduce friction anxiety
